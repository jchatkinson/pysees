A time series gives the load factor over (pseudo-)time. Load patterns refer to one by ID, or define a simple one inline. Series are in the **Time Series** group.

Available types: Constant, Linear, Trig, Triangle, Rectangular, Pulse and Path.

Carapace runs **Constant**, **Linear** and **Path**. Others are exported but stop a Carapace run with an error.

The **Path** type shows the ground-motion importer. See [Ground motion records](/docs/model/ground-motion-input).
