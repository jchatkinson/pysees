The **MP Constraints** group stores `equalDOF`, `equalDOF_Mixed`, `rigidDiaphragm` and `rigidLink` constraints. They are saved in projects and written to exported scripts.

> [!WARNING]
> The Carapace compiler does not apply multi-point constraints yet. Run those models in OpenSees from the exported script.
