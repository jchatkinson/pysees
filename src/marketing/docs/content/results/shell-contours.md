---
title: Shell contours
description: Colour plots of shell stress resultants.
order: 4
keywords: shell contour stress resultants Nx Ny Mxy Qx
---

With shells in a 3D model, **Shell stress resultants** colours the shells by one of eight resultants: Nx, Ny, Nxy, Mx, My, Mxy, Qx and Qy. Values are per unit length in the shell's local axes.

Gauss-point values are extrapolated to the corners and averaged at shared nodes. The scale runs blue, cyan, green, yellow, red. Tick **Plot on deformed shape** to draw the contour on the deformed mesh.
