---
title: Step playback
description: Step or animate through the recorded response.
order: 2
keywords: playback slider fps loop smooth animation
---

Use the step slider and the first, previous, play, next and last buttons to move through steps. Options:

- **Speed** in frames per second.
- **Loop playback**.
- **Smooth (interpolate between steps)** for a continuous animation.

Mode shapes animate separately. See [Mode shapes](/docs/results/mode-shapes).
