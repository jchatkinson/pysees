The three-bar truss with its bars made of concrete with tension softening (an elastic chord keeps the truss stable), pushed down at the apex under displacement control until the material passes its first break point.

Each material in Carapace is its own implementation of the OpenSees model. A single monotonic push checks the backbone; the cyclic cases check the hysteresis rules.
