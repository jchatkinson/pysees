A one-bay, one-story reinforced concrete portal with pinned bases, run through the template's full sequence: eigen analysis, gravity held constant, 20 s of the LOS000 record at 0.1 g scale with Rayleigh damping anchored to the first and third modes, a reset, gravity again and a pushover.

Nonlinear material response under a ground motion, then again after a reset, checks the transient integrator and that each analysis starts from the right state.
