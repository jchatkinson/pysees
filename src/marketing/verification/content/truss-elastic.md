---
title: 2D elastic truss
description: A three-bar truss under an inclined apex load, with elastic bars.
features: Truss, Elastic material
---

A triangular truss (4 m span, 3 m high) with one pinned and one roller support carries a 100 kN vertical and 20 kN horizontal load at its apex. The bars use the `Elastic` uniaxial material.

Truss bars report global end forces in OpenSees, and Carapace reports the same. This case is the baseline for the truss material cases below.
