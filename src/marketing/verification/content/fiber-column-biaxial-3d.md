---
title: Biaxial fiber column with torsion
description: An RC column under constant axial load, pushed at 45° with a torque.
features: DispBeamColumn 3D, biaxial bending, -GJ torsion, Legendre integration
---

A 3 m, 400 × 600 mm reinforced concrete column in three 3D fiber elements carries a 1.5 MN axial load, held, then is pushed with equal X and Y forces and a torque at its top.

The section is stronger about one axis than the other, so the moment interaction path (second plot) curves as the fibers yield. Torsion is carried by the section's elastic `-GJ`.
