---
title: The elastica: large-deflection cantilever
description: A cantilever bent by a tip load until its tip deflects 81% of its length, against the classical elastica.
features: Corotational transformation, large displacements
---

A cantilever with EI = 1 and L = 1, in ten corotational elements, carries a tip load perpendicular to its undeformed axis, increased to PL²/EI = 10 in fifty steps. The tip moves down 0.81 L and in 0.55 L.

Both engines follow the classical elastica (Mattiasson's tabulated solution, dashed) and agree with each other to 1e-6 along the whole path.
