---
title: Cyclic Concrete01
description: A unit bar of Concrete01 driven through growing reversals.
features: Truss, Concrete01, cyclic protocol
---

A bar of unit length and area, so displacement is strain and force is stress, is driven through cycles of growing amplitude by successive displacement-controlled pushes, each continuing from where the last one stopped. The material shows compression with crushing and no tension: the bar unloads to zero stress and reloads.

A soft elastic bar in parallel (a thousandth of the stiffness) keeps the one-degree-of-freedom system from going singular where the material's tangent is zero. The plot shows the material bar's own force.
