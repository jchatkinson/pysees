---
title: 3D fiber frame pushover
description: A two-story RC fiber frame with mixed column orientations, beam element loads and Lobatto integration.
features: DispBeamColumn 3D, vecxz, element loads, Lobatto integration
---

A one-bay by one-bay, two-story reinforced concrete frame. Every member is a 3D fiber element with five-point Lobatto integration, and the columns alternate between two `vecxz` orientations. The beams carry uniform loads in two local directions.

Gravity is held, then the frame is pushed with forces in X and Y. Mixed orientations and element loads on fiber members are the point of this case.
