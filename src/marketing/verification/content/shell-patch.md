---
title: Shell patch on a tilted plane
description: Two distorted MITC4 shells on an inclined plane, clamped on one edge, with general nodal loads.
features: ShellMITC4, ElasticMembranePlateSection, stress resultants
---

Two deliberately distorted quadrilateral `ShellMITC4` elements lie on the tilted plane z = 0.3x + 0.1y, clamped at two nodes and loaded at two others with forces and moments in every direction.

Distortion and the tilted plane exercise the shell's local frame, membrane–bending coupling and drilling stiffness. Displacements, reactions, element end forces and the membrane, bending and shear resultants at every Gauss point are compared.
