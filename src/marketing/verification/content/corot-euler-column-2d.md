---
title: Euler column past buckling
description: A pinned column with a small imperfection, driven past its Euler load into post-buckling.
features: Corotational transformation, displacement control, buckling
---

A pinned column (EI = 1, L = 1) with an L/1000 sine-shaped imperfection is loaded axially. Its mid-height sway is the displacement-control variable, so the analysis passes through the Euler load π²EI/L² and into the post-buckled branch.

The dashed line is the exact elastica of a pinned strut, P/Pcr = (2K(k)/π)² against δ/L = k/K(k). Both engines approach it once the imperfection no longer matters.
