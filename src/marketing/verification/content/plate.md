The PySees plate template: a 4 × 2 MITC4 mesh, clamped along one short edge, loaded by its self-weight and a 5 kPa imposed pressure in two patterns, ramped in four steps.

Plate bending dominates here, so it complements the shell patch cases, which mix membrane and bending action.
