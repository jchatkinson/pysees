title: 3D steel fiber frame under an earthquake
description: A two-story steel fiber frame (Steel02 tubes and I-beams) under both components of a ground motion.
features: DispBeamColumn 3D, Steel02 fibers, Newmark, two ground-motion components
---

The same frame geometry built in steel: square-tube columns and I-beams as Steel02 fiber sections. With floor masses, it runs 6 s of the LOS000 record in X (0.5 scale) and a scaled copy in Y (0.35), with Rayleigh damping.

The frame yields during the record. Steel02's smooth Menegotto–Pinto curve lets both engines converge to a tight tolerance at every step, so the comparison measures the engines and not where each stopped iterating.
