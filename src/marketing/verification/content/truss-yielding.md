The three-bar truss with its bars made of a bilinear steel with 2% hardening, pushed down at the apex under displacement control until the material passes its first break point.

Each material in Carapace is its own implementation of the OpenSees model. A single monotonic push checks the backbone; the cyclic cases check the hysteresis rules.
