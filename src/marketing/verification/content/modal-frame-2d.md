---
title: Modal analysis: 2D frame
description: Frequencies and mode shapes of a three-story, two-bay frame with mass on the translations only.
features: eigen, massless rotations
---

The 2D frame template (three stories, two bays) with 100 kg on both translations of every free node and no rotational mass. The first four modes are compared.

Massless rotations are condensed out of the eigenproblem. The mode shapes are compared at every degree of freedom, rotations included, after normalising each mode by its largest component.
