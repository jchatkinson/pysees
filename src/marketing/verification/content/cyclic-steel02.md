---
title: Cyclic Steel02
description: A unit bar of Steel02 driven through growing reversals.
features: Truss, Steel02, cyclic protocol
---

A bar of unit length and area, so displacement is strain and force is stress, is driven through cycles of growing amplitude by successive displacement-controlled pushes, each continuing from where the last one stopped. The material shows the Menegotto–Pinto curve with the Bauschinger effect.

A soft elastic bar in parallel (a thousandth of the stiffness) keeps the one-degree-of-freedom system from going singular where the material's tangent is zero. The plot shows the material bar's own force.
