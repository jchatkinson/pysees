The 3D frame template (2 × 1 bays, two stories, Z-up) with floor masses runs eigen, gravity, both horizontal components of the LOS000/LOS270 records together (X and Y, 0.1 g scale), a reset, gravity again and a pushover.

Two simultaneous ground-motion directions and a torsionally irregular plan exercise the 3D transient path.
