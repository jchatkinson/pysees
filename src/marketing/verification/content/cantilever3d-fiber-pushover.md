title: 3D fiber cantilever pushover
description: A 3D reinforced concrete fiber column pushed in one direction.
features: DispBeamColumn 3D, fiber section, -GJ, Legendre integration

A 4 m column of two 3D `dispBeamColumn` elements on a rectangular concrete fiber section with four bars and a `-GJ` torsional stiffness, pushed in X under displacement control.

This is the simplest 3D fiber case. The others in this section add biaxial bending, torsion, frames and dynamics.
