---
title: P-Delta portal frame
description: An elastic portal under heavy gravity load, pushed sideways with P-Delta columns: a known formulation difference.
features: PDelta transformation, known difference
---

An elastic portal (3.5 m high, 6 m bay) with P-Delta columns and a linear beam carries 1.5 MN on each column. Gravity is held, then the frame is pushed sideways.

As in the P-Delta cantilever, the engines differ by the P-δ term Carapace includes and OpenSees' `PDelta` leaves out. Here the difference is a few percent of the base shear at a heavy axial load.
