---
title: Cyclic ENT
description: A unit bar of ENT driven through growing reversals.
features: Truss, ENT, cyclic protocol
---

A bar of unit length and area, so displacement is strain and force is stress, is driven through cycles of growing amplitude by successive displacement-controlled pushes, each continuing from where the last one stopped. The material shows elastic in compression, no tension.

A soft elastic bar in parallel (a thousandth of the stiffness) keeps the one-degree-of-freedom system from going singular where the material's tangent is zero. The plot shows the material bar's own force.
